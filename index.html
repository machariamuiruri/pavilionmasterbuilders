Mixx · Cement with Style

Decorative Micro-Cement

Mixx delivers the look of Venetian plaster — the complicated, expensive, high-skill finish — with a low-cost, easy-to-apply product. Two coats, a sealer, and you're done. Water-based, non-hazardous and designed for Africa.

Walls · Floors · Worktops Indoor & Outdoor Wet & Dry Areas Over Existing Tiles Non-Hazardous

Designer Wall

Interior Wall

Applies to newly plastered or painted walls, countertops and ceilings — even wet areas like showers. Complete film thickness 1.4–1.6mm.

12.5kg Plaster + 5L Binder = 35 sqm (2 coats). Seal with 2 coats Silly Seal; add Supreme Seal for wet areas.

Easy Floor

Interior Floor

For tiled floors, new screed or previously coated floors. Hard-wearing with a complete film thickness of 1.4–1.8mm.

12.5kg Plaster + 5L Binder = 35 sqm (2 coats). Seal with 2 coats Serious Seal; add Supreme Seal for wet areas.

Outdoor Plaster

Exterior Wall

A durable external finishing coat with a beautiful mottled appearance. Use on newly plastered or previously painted walls.

25kg Plaster + 9L Water — no binder. Seal with 2 coats Silly Seal.

Designer Colour Range

The Sealers

Silly Seal

For “Designer Wall” finishes.

Serious Seal

For “Easy Floor” finishes.

Supreme Seal

For wet areas such as showers.
